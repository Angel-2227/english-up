Bio Challenge — Read & Answer 📰
⏱ 15 min
📖 Read this real biography of Ed Sheeran. Then answer the WH Questions below.

Edward Christopher Sheeran is a British singer, songwriter, and guitarist. He is from Framingham Earl, a small village in Suffolk, England. He is 33 years old.

Ed was born on February 17, 1991. When he was young, he was very shy and he was not popular at school. He was also not a great student — his real passion was music.

His parents are John and Imogen Sheeran. His father is an art consultant and his mother is a former jewellery designer. Ed's brother is Matthew Sheeran, a music producer.

Today, Ed is one of the best-selling music artists in the world. He is married to Cherry Seaborn, his childhood girlfriend. They are the parents of two daughters. His favourite topics in songs are love, family, and memories.

❓ Who is he? (full name + nationality)
❓ Where is he from?
❓ What is his job?
❓ How old is he?
❓ What was he like when he was young? (2 things)

4
Grammar — Verb To Be: The Full Picture ⭐
⏱ 14 min
In Session 1 we used Verb To Be to describe characters. Now we use it to describe ourselves. Today we master 3 things you need every day: negatives, questions, and short answers.
🎵 Verb To Be — found in "Perfect"
"I am dancing in the dark" → I + am "You are my everything" → You/We/They + are "We were just kids" → past! are → were "Baby, I am not sure" → negative: I'm not…
📌 Positive · Negative · Question — all in one table
Subject✅ Positive❌ Negative❓ Question
II am from Colombia.I am not from Spain. (= I'm not)Am I right?
He / SheShe is fearless.She isn't shy.Is she fearless?
You / We / TheyThey are in love.They aren't fighting.Are they dancing?
Past: I/He/SheHe was shy.He wasn't confident.Was he shy?
Past: TheyThey were kids.They weren't adults.Were they in love?
💡 Short answers — very important!
"Is she fearless?" → "Yes, she is." / "No, she isn't." ✅
"Is she fearless?" → "Yes, she is fearless." ❌ — don't repeat the adjective!
"Are you from Medellín?" → "Yes, I am." / "No, I'm not." ✅
Questions: move Verb To Be to the front. "She is shy." → "Is she shy?"

Pronunciation Drill — Sound Like a Native 🔊
⏱ 8 min
🗣️ Tap any phrase to hear it. Then repeat out loud — 3 times each. Focus on the rhythm, not just the words!
🎯 Today's patterns: Questions & Negatives with Verb To Be
📍 Pattern 1 — Present questions (rising intonation ↗)
🔊 "Are you from Colombia?"
🔊 "Is she confident?"
🔊 "Am I right?"
🔊 "Are they in love?"
📍 Pattern 2 — Past questions (rising intonation ↗)
🔊 "Was he shy?"
🔊 "Were they happy?"
🔊 "Was she from England?"
📍 Pattern 3 — Negatives + contractions (smooth, fast)
🔊 "She isn't shy."
🔊 "He wasn't confident."
🔊 "They weren't fighting."
🔊 "I'm not sure."
📍 Pattern 4 — Short answers (quick, natural)
🔊 "Yes, she is. / No, she isn't."
🔊 "Yes, I am. / No, I'm not."
🔊 "Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't."
🎵 Rhythm tip:
"Are you from Co-LOM-bia?" — stress the 2nd syllable of Colombia
"She IS-n't shy." — the contraction blends: "iz-znt"
"We were JUST kids" — "were" is weak, "just" and "kids" are strong
💡 Try saying each one faster each time you repeat it — that's how fluency builds!

Journalist Role-Play 🎭
⏱ 12 min
🎤 One of you is the JOURNALIST. The other is a CELEBRITY. Switch after 5 minutes!
🎬 The Scenario
You are a famous magazine doing a feature called "This Is Me". A journalist interviews a rising star from Colombia. Use everything you know: name, age, hometown, personality, past, present. All questions and answers must use Verb To Be!
🎙️ Journalist — ask these:
  • Who are you?
  • Where are you from?
  • How old are you?
  • What are you like? (adj.)
  • Were you shy as a child?
  • Are you happy right now?
  • What is your dream?
⭐ Celebrity — answer:
  • My name is…
  • I am from…
  • I am … years old.
  • I am… / I am not…
  • Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
  • Yes, I am! / No, I'm not.
  • My dream is to…
