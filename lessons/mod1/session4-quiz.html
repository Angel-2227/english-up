📄 Text A — A message from Camila

My Favourite Day of the Week

My name is Camila. I am 24 years old and I am from Bogotá, but now I live in Medellín. My favourite day of the week is Saturday.

On Saturday mornings, I don't work. I love to sleep until 9 o'clock. Then I make a big breakfast — my favourite is eggs and bread with a strong cup of coffee. After breakfast, I usually go for a walk in El Poblado with my dog, Mango. Mango is a small, funny, yellow dog. He is very energetic!

In the afternoon, I call my family in Bogotá. My mother is a teacher and my father is a doctor. I have one brother — his name is Sebastián. He is brave and kind. He works in a hospital too.

At night, I like to watch a film or listen to music. I love soul music. Songs like "Killing Me Softly" make me feel calm and nostalgic. I don't like loud music at all. Saturday is perfect.

Writing Tasks
Two writing tasks. There are no wrong topics — write what is true for you. Your teacher will grade these. Focus on using the language from our classes: Verb To Be, adjectives, likes/dislikes, and WH questions.
🎯 25 points (graded by teacher)
Writing Task 1 — Personal Profile (12 points)
Write a short paragraph (8–12 sentences) about yourself. Include:
  • Your name, age, and where you are from
  • What you are like (use at least 3 adjectives)
  • 3 things you love or like doing
  • 1 thing you don't like
  • One sentence about a song that you love and how it makes you feel
💡 Tip: Use Verb To Be correctly (I am / I'm not / Are you...?). Try to use adjectives in the right order.
0 words (aim for 8–12 sentences)
Writing Task 2 — Character Description (13 points)
Think about your favourite character from The Book of Life — or any film you love. Write a short description (6–10 sentences).
  • Who is the character? What is their name?
  • What are they like? Use at least 4 adjectives (in the right order if you use 2+)
  • What does the character like or love doing?
  • Why do you like this character?
💡 Tip: "She is a brave, young woman." · "He is kind and funny." · "I love this character because he is…"
