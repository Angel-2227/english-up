Likes, Loves & Hates 💜❤️💔
⏱ 15 min
Mr Bear loves honey. That's his favourite!

In English, we use like / love / hate to talk about things we enjoy or dislike. They follow a simple pattern — but there's one important rule: what comes after?
📌 The two patterns
PatternExampleNotes
like + noun"I like honey."A thing or general idea
like + verb-ing"I like swimming."An activity → add -ing
love + noun"She loves music."Stronger than like
love + verb-ing"He loves eating honey."Don't use 'to': ✅ eating ❌ to eat
hate + noun"I hate bees."Strong dislike
hate + verb-ing"She hates being alone."Works exactly the same way
💡 Quick rule:
After like/love/hate, use a noun (a thing) OR a verb + -ing (an action).
✅ "I love dancing."  ·  ✅ "I love music."
❌ "I love to dance" — avoid this at A1 level. Use -ing!

Grammar — 3rd Person -s Rules 📐
⏱ 20 min
In the story: "Mr Bear loves honey." — Why loves and not love?

In English, Present Simple verbs change when the subject is he / she / it. We add -s, -es, or sometimes change the spelling. This is called the 3rd person singular rule.
📹 Watch this short explanation (3 min):
📌 The 4 rules
Rule 1 — Most verbs: just add -s
like → likes  ·  love → loves  ·  eat → eats  ·  work → works
Rule 2 — Verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z, -o: add -es
watch → watches  ·  go → goes  ·  do → does  ·  wash → washes  ·  fix → fixes
Memory trick: "She cha-ch-sh-x-z-oses" → those endings get -es!
Rule 3 — Verbs ending in consonant + y: change y → ies
study → studies  ·  fly → flies  ·  cry → cries  ·  try → tries
BUT: vowel + y → just add -s  → play → plays  ·  enjoy → enjoys
Rule 4 — Irregular: have → has  ·  be → is
He has a dog. (not "haves")  ·  She is happy. (not "bes")
Base verbRuleHe/She/ItExample
like+slikesShe likes coffee.
love+slovesHe loves dancing.
watch+eswatchesShe watches movies.
go+esgoesHe goes to work.
studyy→iesstudiesShe studies English.
play+s (vowel+y)playsHe plays guitar.
haveirregularhasShe has a cat.

📝 Exam Checklist — Am I Ready? ✅
⏱ 7 min
Tap each topic to check it off. If you're not confident, use the hint in blue to review.
□
✅ Verb To Be — present + past
am / is / are · was / were · positive · negative · questions
Example: "She is brave." · "Were they friends?"
□
✅ WH Questions
Who / What / Where / When / Why / How / How old
Example: "Where are you from?" · "What is your favourite colour?"
□
✅ Adjectives + order
Personality (brave, kind, funny…) · Size · Age → OPINION → SIZE → AGE
Example: "He is a brave young man."
□
✅ Likes / Loves / Hates + noun or -ing
I like music. · She loves dancing. · He hates bees.
Example: "I love cooking." (NOT "I love to cook" at A1)
□
✅ 3rd Person -s rules
+s · +es (ch/sh/x/z/o) · consonant+y → ies · have→has · be→is
Example: "She teaches." · "He tries." · "She has a cat."
□
✅ Do / Does questions + short answers
"Do you like…?" / "Does she like…?" · "Yes, I do." / "No, she doesn't."
Example: "Does she study English? Yes, she does."
□
✅ Self-presentation in 5+ sentences
Name · Age · Origin · Description · Likes
Example: "I am María. I am 32 years old. I am from Medellín. I am friendly and creative. I love music."
