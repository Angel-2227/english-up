Verb To Be — Full Review ⭐
⏱ 20 min
⭐
Verb To Be = am / is / are / was / were
We use Verb To Be to say WHO someone is, WHAT they are like, WHERE they are from, and HOW they feel. It changes with the subject and the time.
📌 Complete Reference Table — tap 🔊 on each example to hear it!
Subject✅ Positive❌ Negative❓ Question
I I am from Colombia. I 'm not from Spain. Am I right?
He / She / It She is a teacher. He isn't tired. Is she happy?
You / We / They They are friends. We aren't late. Are they ready?
I / He / She (past) He was shy. She wasn't happy. Was she nervous?
You / We / They (past) They were young. We weren't ready. Were they there?
💡 Short answers: "Is she happy?" → "Yes, she is." / "No, she isn't." — never repeat the full sentence!
💡 Contractions: am not = 'm not  ·  is not = isn't  ·  are not = aren't  ·  was not = wasn't  ·  were not = weren't

Present Simple — Habits & Routines 📅
⏱ 25 min
📅
Present Simple = things you do regularly
Use for habits, routines, facts, and things that are generally true. Key words: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week, on Mondays…
📌 Rules — positive, negative, questions + the -s rule
Subject✅ Positive❌ Negative❓ Question
I / You / We / They I work every day. I don't work on Sunday. Do you work here?
He / She / It She works at a school. She doesn't work at night. Does she work here?
💡 The -s rule (3rd person He/She/It): Always add -s to the verb!
✅ She likes coffee. ✅ He reads every night. ✅ She watches TV.
❌ She like coffee. ← WRONG! Missing -s!

💡 Spelling: -ch/-sh/-x/-o → add -es (watches, goes)  ·  consonant + y → -ies (study → studies)
AdverbFrequencyExample
always100%She always drinks coffee in the morning.
usually~80%He usually walks to work.
often~60%We often watch movies on Friday.
sometimes~40%I sometimes cook at home.
rarely~20%She rarely eats fast food.
never0%He never goes to bed before midnight.

Present Continuous — Happening Right Now 🎬
⏱ 20 min
🎬
Present Continuous = am/is/are + verb-ing
Use for actions happening RIGHT NOW. Key words: now, right now, at the moment, look!, listen!, today, this week.
📌 How to form it + spelling rules
Subject✅ Positive❌ Negative❓ Question
I I am working. I 'm not working. Am I working?
He / She / It She is sleeping. She isn't sleeping. Is she sleeping?
You / We / They They are dancing. They aren't dancing. Are they dancing?
💡 Spelling rules for -ing:
• Most verbs: just add -ing → work → working, eat → eating
• Verb ends in -e: drop the -e, add -ing → dance → dancing, write → writing
• Short verb (1 vowel + 1 consonant): double the consonant → run → running, sit → sitting, swim → swimming

💡 PS vs PC difference:
• "She reads every night." ← HABIT (Present Simple)
• "She is reading right now." ← HAPPENING NOW (Present Continuous)
